# Personagens — "Podcast: Jesus vs. Diabo" (estilo 3D cinematográfico)

Estilo-base para TODAS as imagens (cole no início de cada prompt, para manter a consistência):

> **STYLE:** stylized 3D animated feature film still, high-end family animation studio look, soft subsurface skin shading, expressive large eyes, appealing proportions, ultra-detailed hair and fabric simulation, cinematic lighting, shallow depth of field, anamorphic lens, volumetric light, 8k render, global illumination, film grain subtle

Negativo (se a ferramenta aceitar *negative prompt*):

> `realistic photo, uncanny, creepy realism, gore, blood, deformed hands, extra fingers, text, watermark, logo, flat 2D, sketch, lineart, black and white, low poly, blurry face`

---

## 1. JESUS

**Personalidade visual:** sereno, acolhedor, sábio, com um sorriso leve. Ele nunca se exalta: responde com calma e firmeza. É a luz da cena.

**Ficha fixa (repita igual em todo prompt):**
- homem de ~33 anos, pele morena-oliva do Oriente Médio, olhos castanho-mel grandes e gentis
- cabelo castanho-escuro ondulado na altura dos ombros, barba cheia e bem cuidada
- túnica de linho off-white com faixa/manto bordô sobre um ombro, tecido com textura visível
- **sem** coroa de espinhos no estúdio (opcional: usar só em cenas de flashback)
- luz quente dourada (rim light) atrás dele, halo sutil, **nunca** exagerado
- paleta: branco-creme, dourado, bordô, madeira clara

### Prompt 1 — Retrato oficial (character sheet / referência principal)
```
STYLE + Character portrait of Jesus as a stylized 3D animated movie character, 33 year old Middle Eastern man, warm olive skin, large kind honey-brown eyes, shoulder-length wavy dark brown hair, full neat beard, gentle confident smile, off-white linen tunic with a deep burgundy sash over one shoulder, visible fabric weave. Soft golden rim light from behind, subtle warm glow, clean dark-teal gradient background, front 3/4 view, chest-up, centered, 16:9
```

### Prompt 2 — Jesus no podcast (cena principal)
```
STYLE + Jesus as a stylized 3D animated movie character sitting at a premium podcast studio desk, speaking calmly into a professional broadcast microphone on a boom arm, studio headphones resting on his neck, hands gently open in an explaining gesture. Warm walnut wood desk, soft golden key light from the right, warm practical lamps and bokeh string lights in the background, acoustic panels in cream and gold, faint god rays through haze. Medium shot, eye level, 35mm lens, shallow depth of field, 16:9
```

### Prompt 3 — Expressões (gerar variações com a mesma seed / referência)
Troque só a parte da expressão:
- `serene closed-mouth smile, looking at camera`
- `compassionate look, slight head tilt, listening`
- `soft laugh, eyes squinting kindly`
- `firm and serious, calm authority, one finger raised gently`
- `sad and compassionate, eyes slightly glossy`

## 2. O DIABO

**Personalidade visual:** carismático, sarcástico, manipulador, "vendedor charmoso" — engraçado, nunca assustador demais (público família). É a sombra da cena.

**Ficha fixa (repita igual em todo prompt):**
- pele vermelho-carmim com leve brilho, rosto anguloso, queixo pontudo, cavanhaque preto afiado
- chifres pretos curvos e brilhantes, orelhas pontudas, olhos âmbar com pupila fina
- sobrancelhas grossas e muito expressivas, sorriso torto com caninos pequenos
- terno preto slim com camisa vermelho-vinho e gravata fina preta, abotoaduras douradas
- cauda vermelha terminando em ponta de flecha, sempre em movimento
- paleta: preto, vermelho, laranja-brasa, detalhes dourados

### Prompt 1 — Retrato oficial (character sheet / referência principal)
```
STYLE + Character portrait of the Devil as a charismatic stylized 3D animated movie villain, glossy crimson red skin, angular face, sharp black goatee, curved shiny black horns, pointed ears, amber eyes with slit pupils, thick expressive eyebrows, sly crooked grin with small fangs, sleek black slim-fit suit with wine-red shirt and thin black tie, gold cufflinks, arrow-tipped red tail curling behind him. Dramatic red and orange rim light, floating embers, dark smoky background, front 3/4 view, chest-up, centered, 16:9
```

### Prompt 2 — Diabo no podcast (cena principal)
```
STYLE + The Devil as a charismatic stylized 3D animated movie villain sitting at a premium podcast studio desk, leaning back confidently, speaking into a professional broadcast microphone on a boom arm, one hand gesturing with a pointed finger, tail curling over the chair. Black marble desk with red LED underglow, moody red and orange key light from the left, glowing ember particles, dark acoustic panels, neon red accent light, smoke haze. Medium shot, eye level, 35mm lens, shallow depth of field, 16:9
```

### Prompt 3 — Expressões
- `sly smirk, one eyebrow raised, looking at camera`
- `fake friendly smile, palms open, "trust me" gesture`
- `angry shouting, teeth showing, pointing finger forward`
- `nervous sweating, forced smile, eyes darting sideways`
- `defeated, slumped, ears down, tail drooping`
